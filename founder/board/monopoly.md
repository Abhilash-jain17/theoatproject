# Monopoly lens
Score: 4/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Distribution already exists.** It starts with a showroom, an existing AJH customer base, a WhatsApp/AskEva list, an app and an in-house content team. The first 100 online buyers can come from people who already trust the house — the cheapest acquisition a jeweller can get.
- **A small market it could own first.** "Chennai women 22–38 who already trust a family jeweller, reached in Tanglish" is narrow enough to dominate. Pan-India silver is not.
- **Brand is the only durable advantage on offer, and AJH has a head start on it.** Hallmark culture and family-jeweller heritage are things a pure online silver start-up cannot copy in month six.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **A commodity product in a crowded, low-barrier category.** 925 silver at ₹1,999–₹2,499 has no proprietary technology, no network effect and no scale advantage over existing sellers. No competitors are named; until /founder-competitors maps them, assume many sell within one click of this buyer.
2. **Going pan-India too early.** Outside South India the AJH name carries little weight; Silver Story becomes another listing buying ads at unknown CAC, burning the ₹15–50 lakh without building a position.
3. **No secret.** The brief does not say what AJH knows about this buyer or this product that others do not. "Heritage plus minimal design" is a positioning claim, not a secret. Nothing stops a copycat.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Who else sells 925 silver at ₹1,500–₹3,000 to women 22–38 in Chennai, online and offline? (/founder-competitors)
- What share of current AJH customers or their daughters would buy silver online, and at what price? (/founder-consumer)
- What does a piece cost to make, what is the gross margin, and what CAC can that margin carry? (/founder-cfo)
- What does AJH know that a pure-online silver brand does not?
- Why now? What changed this year?
- What will Silver Story have in ten years that a well-funded online competitor cannot buy?

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. Launch to Chennai/South India and the existing AJH base first. No pan-India paid acquisition until the brand holds a measurable, leading share of repeat buyers in that home market.
2. The founder names the secret in one sentence, backed by something only AJH has, e.g. showroom tie-ins (try in-store, exchange or upgrade to gold).
3. /founder-competitors shows a gap this brand can own, and /founder-cfo shows the margin per piece covers realistic CAC before marketing spend exceeds about a third of the budget.
