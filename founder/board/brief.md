# Silver Story by Anand Jewel House — online

- What it is: an online (D2C) 925 sterling silver jewellery brand, the younger vertical of Anand Jewel House (AJH), a Chennai family jeweller selling 18K/22K gold, diamonds and silver. Endorsed-brand structure: always "Silver Story by Anand Jewel House".
- Who it is for: women 22–38, buying everyday elegant silver for themselves and as gifts. The brand sees Chennai/South India as its home market and the rest of India as the online reach.
- What it sells, at what price: 925 silver everyday jewellery and gift sets, with price points around ₹1,999 and ₹2,499 (₹1,999 gift sets planned). Brand tagline register: "Silver that speaks before you do."
- Where and how: today silver sells in-store only at the AJH showroom in Chennai (www.anandjewelhouse.in). The plan is to take it online: D2C site/app, Instagram reels (Chennai Tanglish + English), WhatsApp Business list (AskEva) for drops, rate alerts and a free style guide as a freemium entry. Launch-week free shipping and premium gift wrap are planned.
- Budget and constraints: ₹15–50 lakh over 12 months for stock, marketing and tech. Assets already in place: AJH's trust and heritage (BIS hallmark culture), the showroom, an existing customer base, a WhatsApp/AskEva setup, an AJH app, and an in-house content engine. Brand rules: minimal, premium, no cheap discount language, no "up to" offers, no gold-rate discounting; the silver vertical may use ₹1,999/₹2,499-style price points.

## Additional context for the board
- Stage: Silver Story is live in-store only. There are no online sales yet, so online demand, CAC, return rates and repeat rates are all unknown.
- The parent AJH brand positions as "the trusted modern family jeweller": heritage trust with a luxury-minimal, digital-first presentation. Bridal and gold are the core profit engines; Silver Story is the growth and new-customer bet aimed at a younger buyer.
- Margin philosophy at AJH: margin lives in making charges/value addition. Silver Story's price points are product prices, not metal-rate based.
- Not provided (treat as open questions): unit cost and gross margin per piece, online CAC, market size, named competitors, the founder's split of the budget between stock and marketing.
