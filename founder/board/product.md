# Product lens
Score: 5/10 | Vote: FUND IF

## What is strong
- **Trust carried over from AJH.** A family jeweller with a hallmark culture solves the hardest question for anyone buying silver online: "is this real 925?" Most online silver sellers have to earn that. This one already has it.
- **Simple price points.** ₹1,999 and ₹2,499 are product prices, not metal-rate maths. That is the right call for an everyday buyer.
- **The brand rules already show taste.** Minimal, premium, no "up to" offers, no discount noise. A business that refuses cheap language is more likely to sweat the details nobody sees.
- **A showroom to learn from.** Silver Story sells in-store today, so there is a real place to watch what customers pick up, ask about and buy.

## What would kill it (the top 3, most dangerous first)
1. **There is no "one thing" yet.** The brief describes channels (site, app, reels, WhatsApp, rate alerts, style guide, gift wrap) but not the product. What is a Silver Story piece, and why is it better than any other ₹1,999 sterling silver pendant on Instagram? Without a design signature, it is 925 silver at a common price, and the parent brand's trust is the only difference. Who else sells at this price is unknown; /founder-competitors answers it.
2. **Too many surfaces for one small team.** A D2C site and an app on top of the existing AJH app, two-language reels, AskEva drops, rate alerts and a freemium style guide, all on ₹15–50 lakh. Each one is a place to build, maintain and get wrong. Rate alerts don't fit a product priced on design rather than metal rate. Cut to one store and one channel until online buyers are proven.
3. **The seams between the showroom and online.** In-store, the trust comes from a person, a counter and a hallmark you can hold. Online, it has to come from photos, packaging, delivery, sizing, tarnish care and returns. None of these are designed yet, and return and repeat rates are unknown. If a ₹1,999 gift arrives looking like a ₹600 marketplace piece, the AJH name takes the damage.

## The questions I would ask the founder
- Describe Silver Story in one sentence without using the word "silver".
- What does the first five minutes look like for a 26-year-old who orders a gift set: the box, the card, the care instructions, the proof it is 925?
- Which three pieces would you keep if you could sell only three online?
- Does an online order live on the AJH app, a new app or a website, and who answers when it goes wrong?
- What is the unit cost and gross margin per piece, and does premium gift wrap with free shipping still pay? (/founder-cfo)
- Would buyers outside Chennai pay ₹2,499 to a brand they have never seen in person? (/founder-consumer)

## If FUND IF: the conditions
1. A one-sentence product definition and a focused launch range of no more than about 12 pieces, each with a visible design signature.
2. One online storefront and one acquisition channel at launch. No separate app, and no rate alerts for silver.
3. The full unboxing, care, sizing and returns experience designed and tested on real showroom customers before spending on paid marketing.
4. Unit margin confirmed by /founder-cfo and willingness to pay confirmed by /founder-consumer, before stock is committed.
